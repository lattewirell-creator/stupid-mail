S

Sealed, not shared.

  • Passwords, AI keys and your Outlook sign-in live in the macOS Keychain, or encrypted for your Windows account, never in the app’s web view.
  • Your mail, drafts and sorting are cached in private files on your computer, separately for each address.
  • Emails show as text without outside images or tracking scripts, so nobody learns when you opened them.
  • Every email asks for one last confirmation before it goes out.
  • New versions are posted on GitHub and here. Automatic updates are built in and switch on in a later version.
Return to sender

Honest limits

Things it sends back, for now.

  • Big inboxes. It reads your latest 40 inbox messages, not other server folders.
  • Attachments. None yet, for reading or sending. Very large emails may need your provider’s app.
  • A second account. One at a time. Connecting another replaces the saved sign-in, while drafts and sorting are kept per address.
  • Outlook out of the box. For now you need your own free Microsoft app registration. The guide walks you through it.
  • Official seals. It isn’t notarized by Apple or code-signed for Windows, so your computer asks once the first time you open it.

Letters to the editor

The first-time questions, answered.

Dear Stupid Mail,macOS won’t open you. Rude?

It’s because the app isn’t from the App Store or notarized by Apple. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. Only once, promise.

Dear Stupid Mail,Which password do I give you?

Never your normal one. iCloud uses an app-specific password from account.apple.com. Gmail needs 2-Step Verification and an app password. Outlook signs in on Microsoft’s page. The setup guide has every step.

Dear Stupid Mail,How do I wake Buddy up?

Click the gear in Buddy’s panel, pick Ollama, OpenAI or Claude and type a model name. Ollama needs a model installed and running on your computer (at 127.0.0.1:11434). OpenAI and Claude need your own API key.

Dear Stupid Mail,I have an older version.

Install the newest one once, replacing the old app in Applications. From 2.5 on it checks for updates itself and installs them when you quit. Your accounts, drafts and cached mail live outside the app, so they stay.

Dear Stupid Mail,Did my email actually send?

You confirm every send. If the confirmation fails, look in your provider’s Sent folder before trying again, so it doesn’t go twice. “Sent here” in the app is a local list, not your server’s Sent folder.

Dear Stupid Mail,Can I bring two addresses?

Not at the same time yet. Connecting another replaces the saved sign-in. Drafts, reminders and sorting are kept per address for when you switch back.
